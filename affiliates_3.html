Common questions

Still have questions? Contact us directly.

How is Affiliate different from standard Referral?
Referral is open to all Bitval users — no application, public tier ladder. Affiliate is application-based for partners driving traffic at scale, with commission agreed individually at review.
Who qualifies for the Affiliate program?
Creators, publishers, newsletter writers, community operators, and high-volume referrers with an established audience and a credible path to qualified sign-ups. Apply and let the team assess fit.
Is approval instant or does it require review?
Not instant. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and are not automatically approved. You will be contacted with a decision. Approval depends on audience fit, reach, and distribution quality.
How is commission calculated?
Commission is calculated on trading fees paid by referred users on qualifying trades. Rates are agreed during your partnership review, not publicly posted, and paid in USDT as referred users trade.
By applying you agree to the Affiliate Terms. Approval is not guaranteed and is subject to review. Commissions are conditional on eligibility and qualifying activity. Bitval does not offer investment advice.
