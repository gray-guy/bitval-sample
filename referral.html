FAQ

Common questions

If you're running at scale — creators, communities, publishers — the Affiliate program is a better fit. It's a separate track with higher custom rates.

What counts as a qualifying friend?
A friend qualifies once they sign up through your link and complete the required verification and activity steps listed in the Referral Terms. A signup alone does not unlock a reward.
When and how do I get paid?
Referral rewards are credited to your Bitval account after the required conditions are met. You can follow reward events and payout status from your Referrals workspace.
How is Referral different from Affiliate?
Referral is the standard invite-friends track — open to everyday Bitval users who want to share a link or code with friends. Affiliate is an application-based partnership for high-volume referrers such as creators, media, and communities.
Is there a cap on how much I can earn?
There's no cap on invites. Total rewards depend on the current program terms, how many referrals qualify, and the eligible activity they complete.
Are rewards guaranteed?
No. Rewards are conditional on eligibility and qualifying activity as defined in the Referral Terms. Bitval may adjust qualifying events, payout details, or the program itself; changes are announced in advance where possible.
I was invited — what do I get?
If you arrive through a referral link, any active referral offer is shown during signup. Final benefits depend on the current promotion, eligibility checks, and qualifying actions.
